# Characters, events, roleplay and combat

**Player side:** create and manage character sheets, select approved FFXIV-based job progression, join eligible events and use encounters/dice with event/GM oversight. **Staff side:** roster and character approvals, reward administration, encounters and plots.

**Character system:** identity and owner/account, job/class, level and progression are separate from a user's Discord account role. Member rank and character sheet are not the same permission. Character sheet settings and optional roleplay/consent fields must respect privacy and approval rules. Do not share hidden character fields through public directories.

**Progression and dice:** the character progression modules own XP/level/class constraints, while encounter and dice modules own encounter resolution. RP story authority remains with an authorized GM; mechanical dice outcomes do not automatically rewrite territory or override approved war decisions.

**Events:** event planning, signups and character participation are separate from automatic payment/rewards or world settlement changes. Where supported, staff post event outcomes and XP explicitly.

**Roleplay visibility:** material in personal writings or a public scene is not automatically IC knowledge for every character. OOC consent and the relevant event restrictions apply to dark or mature material.
