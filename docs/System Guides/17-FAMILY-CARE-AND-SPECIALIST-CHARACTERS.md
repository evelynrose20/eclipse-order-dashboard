# Character-specialist subsystems and family care

These features are distinct from settlement housing/population. They store **character-level or dependent-level** records, and must not be assumed to create civilian residents, industrial workers or a personal property deed.

**Family Care:** approved accounts can manage authorized dependent records and care actions with logged supplies/status. Bottles or meals require the dependent's recorded milk/food stock; a supply restock is a separate accounted action with a source description. Family-care stock is not automatically the same as civilian food inventory or chamber batches.

**Character reproductive/lineage records:** specialist character pages and projects have separate privacy, eligibility, staff authorization and lifecycle rules. A lineage project's status does not automatically trigger town growth, military recruitment or the creation of another player's character.

**Brood/strain gameplay:** the breeding-pit RP mechanic maintains its own strains, inventory, incubation capacity, maturation and cohorts. Those quantities are not free substitutes for factories, settlement population or combat troops, and status/capacity rules must be followed. Do not expose staff or character-private fields in the public roster.

**Consent/visibility:** any dark or mature RP content requires OOC consent and appropriate access controls. A private character edit should not be treated as automatic player consent or a world-state event.
