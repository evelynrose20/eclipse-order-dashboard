## Vision
Build the Eclipse Order's FFXIV/Garlean RP into a configurable persistent living world, not a separate strategy game. New Cassia sits at the center of a regional and eventually realm-scale territorial, civilian, industrial, political and military simulation. Events, dice encounters, construction, crafting, trades, fronts, factions, settlements, and the Senate change shared world state. **Player-led RP generates outcomes; authorized DMs confirm significant outcomes.** Noncombat members can meaningfully contribute. Campaigns may last weeks or months.

## Hard requirements
1. **Data-driven world builder, no code changes for ordinary content.** User creates custom regions, zones, maps, settlements, sites, industries, facilities, resources, production recipes, fleets, convoys, military formations, vehicles, event chains, objective dependencies and rule templates through an administrative editor. New example content: voidsent-infested ore mine; ceruleum drilling + processing plant with a multi-event capture/repair/production storyline. Reusable templates + individual instances. Feature extension may still require code for fundamentally novel mechanics.
2. **Shared territorial map** for peacetime and war, **linked to existing Factions of the Realm records** (order_story/world/settlement). New Cassia, nearby smaller settlements aided by other governments, allied/neutral/hostile sites, other factions and Order expansion must all be visible. Distinguish **governing faction, military controller, supporting faction(s), economic beneficiary, reputation**. Friendly or funded does not imply territorial ownership. Allow zones/regions, free-placement markers, editable territory polygons, roads/rail/sea routes, uploaded backgrounds, zoom, layers (political, territory, settlements, supply, conflict, intelligence), historical ownership, map visibility by permissions.
3. **Site lifecycle**: occupied/infested → recon and DM-run liberation → securing → reconstruction through existing Projects and crafted supply reservations → workforce/power commissioning → industry production → transport/trade. Facilities may unlock additional events and can later be attacked, damaged, repaired, upgraded or abandoned. Site assets and industry types are configurable.
4. **NPC civilians** tracked in aggregate by settlement with optional named NPC dossiers. Fields: population, workforce, employment, housing, food, water, medicine, heating, fuel/power, public safety, stability/unrest, displacement and relief. Support civilian/public works (roads, hospitals, housing, heating plants, clinics).
5. **Food variety, food diversity and realistic satisfaction**: food items belong to configurable food groups, carry nutrition/variety attributes; count delivered rations, variety across recent cycles/months, affordability/access; monotonous diets reduce satisfaction or increase grievances over time but **must not punish every repeated item or force unrealistic menu micromanagement**. Different population groups may have distinct dietary preferences. Distinguish food quantity from food quality/diversity, shortages from social unrest, and morale from physical health. Monthly rolling variety and minimum essentials are configurable.
6. **Heating and weather**: each region has climate/weather conditions (temperature, rain, snow, severe weather, seasonal conditions), exposure and forecasts/simulation parameters; each settlement has climate-specific heat demand, insulation, housing suitability, fuel stock, heating infrastructure and power availability. Cold snaps/heatwaves alter civilian needs, military logistics, transport route safety, fuel use, industrial output, construction and story hooks. Weather and temperature should be editable for FFXIV lore and DM plots, not rigid real-world API weather. **No automatic deadly consequences**; major events need DM approval.
7. **Unrest and civic politics**: discontent emerges from cumulative unmet needs, monotonous diet, cold homes, damaged services, occupation, harsh policies, employment or unrest-provoking RP. Track unrest/stability as bounded indicators, reasons and thresholds. Unrest can generate optional petitions, strikes, demonstrations, smuggling, disputes, crime/violence RP hooks; don't auto-kill citizens or arbitrarily overthrow factions. Government/public works, food diversity, diplomacy and relief can improve conditions. Alert staff before escalation; DM approves major unrest outcomes.
8. **Economy, industry, crafting, stockpiles**: editable production inputs/outputs, byproducts, capacity, labor, power, maintenance, climate restrictions, completion requirements and cycle modes (manual/narrative, managed reset, detailed simulation). **Never duplicate or secretly confiscate player/front-owned items**. Distinguish personal, reserved and committed resources. Ledgers, idempotent production and atomic transfers.
9. **Trade and transports**: routes, scheduled/DM-controlled NPC convoys, transport templates (Garlean trucks, armored carriers, trains, airships, ships, pack animals), capacity, crews, fuel, escort, travel hazards, cargo reservations, origin/in-transit/destination stockpiles, aid shipments, contracts, approvals, auditing. Goods cannot be available both at departure and arrival.
10. **Military registry**: editable formations, soldiers, readiness, morale, training, weapons, vehicles, tanks, magitek walkers, repairs, stationed location, upkeep/fuel, casualties and replacements subject to DM validation. Aggregate NPC forces; named units only when valuable. Armies depend on real resource supply and territory routes.
11. **Hybrid wars**: Domina declares/ends official wars; appointed campaign commander coordinates; event DM runs operations; members participate; Senate debates funding/war policy if convened; Domina retains reserved authorization. Campaign phases: draft, mobilization, active, armistice/negotiation, resolution, archived (+ paused). Operations reuse Events and Encounters, offer success/partial/failure/complication, contribute proposals, grant territory ownership, supplies, alliances or further objectives only on approved major outcome. Draft, preview, audit, revise/correct workflows, no duplicate commit after restart.
12. **Senate**: 15 hybrid-elected seats confirmed by Domina + 3 Heads, first is Domina. Senate disabled until Domina convenes; Council otherwise governs. Political party system already exists. Votes and proposals don't execute treasury spending or territorial changes automatically. Linked war appropriations and treaties require authorized execution.
13. **Dashboard/navigation redesign**: current flat navigation will be too crowded; group under coherent hubs (**World Administration**, **Operations / War Command**, **Economy & Logistics**, **Government**, **Characters / Community**) with collapsible nested sections, search, permission-aware access, clear staff/player experience. Keep Garlean gunmetal/crimson/brass theme. Avoid modern generic bright UI, inaccessible tiny elements, overwhelming page lists.
14. **Access/consent/safety**: RP bad-end themes remain OOC consensual. IC authorities never override actual moderation, account security or OOC consent. Don’t apply automatic character punishment from simulated events. Non-staff may inspect only public/published/known records. Secret intelligence must not leak via read APIs.
15. **Simulation defaults**: disabled or narrative-first until explicitly enabled per settlement/campaign; human-configurable thresholds, caps, grace periods; avoid offline punitive cascades. DM-approved significant permanent consequences. Audit all changes. World starts empty: no fabricated canon settlements, faction relationships or population numbers seeded.

## Proposed architecture
- Existing sources of truth: order_factions, faction reputation and events; existing Event Board, Encounters and Dice; Construction Projects; crafting/workshop inventories; Commerce/Trade; Order fronts, Treasury; Senate.
- Add normalized world regions, map layers and site records referencing order_factions.id; territory polygon geometry and route graph; civilian population groups and needs snapshots; food categories, food-consumption deliveries and rolling variety; weather/climate snapshots; facilities and industry templates/instances; production recipes and ledgers; registered NPC units and vehicles; convoys and manifests; campaigns/operations/outcome proposals; resource-transfer ledger; audit.
- Keep rules **structured declarative JSON** with validated condition/action allowlists, versions, dry-run simulation, approval queue, cycle detection and idempotent transaction keys. No arbitrary executable JS/SQL in public editors.
- Authorized writes transactionally commit once; conflicts compare saved versions. Material source IDs and historical events remain stable across updates.

## Practical implementation phases
**Foundation 1 (first deliverable):** save this master plan in repo, add collapsible World Administration hub, additive DB for regions and settlements linked to factions, basic civic/food/heating/weather/unrest profile and editable read-only preview. No automatic ticks or fabricated data; ability to create and edit a settlement and region safely through API.
**Foundation 2:** interactive editable map with site markers, polygon borders, ownership and faction support relationships; climate zone and faction records.
**Foundation 3:** configurable food definitions/deliveries, diet variety rolling windows, heat demand and fuel system, controlled/previewable simulation ticks, unrest alerts/DM-approved incidents.
**Foundation 4:** site/facility/item/industry templates, full rule builder, crafting+construction integration, productive mine/ceruleum plant acceptance flows.
**Foundation 5:** trade routes, transports/convoy cargo and workforce, civilian consumption and public works.
**Foundation 6:** army/unit/vehicle registry, formations and military supply.
**Foundation 7:** full War Command campaigns, events, maps, outcome approvals, diplomacy and Senate permissions.
**UX iteration:** progressively regroup all navigation into scalable hubs; remove redundancies and ensure each new section is discoverable.

B. Create a voidsent-occupied mine, write linked recon/liberation events, approve its capture, build repair projects requiring crafted supplies, resume staffed ore extraction and ship ore to New Cassia.
C. Create ceruleum drilling/processing complex from configurable templates; capture, rebuild, enable multiple-stage production, transport refined fuel, supply tanks; no code changes.
D. Create simulated civilians; expose them to an unusually cold month, inadequate heating fuel and repeated monotonous rations; show warnings, falling satisfaction and unrest **only in controlled simulations**, and allow relief deliveries, diverse food supply and heating repairs to restore stability. Unrest does not cause irreversible harm without DM approval.

F. Inactive Senate and no active war: all existing Council and event systems still work; default world simulation does not modify legacy records.

## Update — Garlean weather and visual roads (2026-11-07)
- Real-world **calendar** dates now drive a deterministic fictional Garlean climate, with intensely cold/snowy winters and relatively mild-to-warm spring/summer/autumn. Temperature is displayed in **Fahrenheit**. It is NOT a live weather report for a real city. Existing Celsius storage feeds heating calculations internally; DM can override seasonal weather by selecting manual mode. Climate does not automatically advance food consumption or unrest.
- Map markers are assigned configurable icon categories (capital, village, mine, refinery, factory, outpost, fortress, port, depot, ruins, etc.). Map editing lets staff drag and persist positions without replacing unrelated civilian record fields.
- Region road records have two settlement endpoints and optional editable map bends; types include road, trail, rail, skyway and waterway. Road links are visual and foundational: no automated distance, ETA, or convoy shipment pathfinding yet.
- World Administration's selectors use the rest of the portal's shared dropdown theme.
- Migration: `server/sql/042_world_routes_garlean_weather.sql`; UI: `src/WorldTerritoryMap.jsx`; climate model: `server/src/garlean-climate.js`.
- **Still planned:** real territory polygon drawing, multi-layer political borders, weather-adjusted logistics, route graph/pathfinding, transport scheduling, industrial production, monthly diet diversity based on actual food-group deliveries, simulation ticks and DM approvals.

## Update — Territory state, mountain ranges and Order authority (2026-11-07)
- All sites now display an always-visible state-colored badge: peaceful (green), contested (amber), occupied (red), liberated (teal) and rebuilding (blue). The selection halo does not hide the actual status. Command can set a site state directly from map controls.
- Create named mountain ranges by placing a sequence of ridge points on the map. Existing ranges can be selected, redrawn and renamed or deleted. They are geographic overlays, not actual changes of territory ownership, and do not automatically block roads.
- The first-party `eclipse-order` entry is inserted idempotently into the existing `order_factions` registry by migration 043. It can be chosen as a Government, Control, or Supporting faction without special-case fake IDs. Existing settlement ownership is not modified by migration.
- Residents replaces NPC residents in the settlement editor, inspector and registry. Storage remains `population` for existing compatibility.
- New schema: `043_world_mountain_ranges_order_faction.sql`; full setup requires a backup, `npm run setup:local` from `server/`, and a Windows API restart.
- Still to implement: true territory ownership polygons, terrain elevation and impassable mountain ranges with pathfinding, new site types and proper simulated settlement population/industry/war flows.

## Update — Site conditions and railway/road cartography (2026-11-07)
- **Ownership is not a condition.** A territory can legally belong to the Eclipse Order through the controlling/governing faction reference while its site condition is **Secure**, **Abandoned**, **Destroyed**, **Occupied**, **Contested**, etc. The Order's own holdings are not automatically occupied. True territory polygons/borders remain future work.
- Settlement conditions `secure`, `abandoned`, and `destroyed` are added by idempotent schema migration `044_world_site_conditions.sql`, retaining all existing states and ownership references.
- Route creation now has a draft stage: select endpoints, place manual bends, review and explicitly save or cancel; clicking the second site must not immediately create an unwanted line. Existing routes are selected through a route registry; edits to bends/type/name are saved explicitly. Rail routes draw recognizable sleepers and twin rails instead of dotted roads. Routes remain visual; no automated transport movement yet.
- Further road upgrades: shared intersections and rail junctions, station/track metadata, route segment splitting, road elevation, routing, crossings, and convoy pathfinding.

## Update — Railway and trade infrastructure (November 2026)
The user explicitly deferred political ownership polygons/borders and secret/public map layers because the active territory maps are small. The next priority is usable railway freight infrastructure on the existing smooth map. Migration 047 adds stations anchored to rail nodes, freight and passenger platforms, branch-switch track pair restrictions, and road/rail crossings. The read-only freight route preview checks whether freight-ready stations are connected through open switches and operational crossings, but **does not** dispatch trains or move inventory. Users can convert one older road/rail line into the graph at a time without changing other routes. Full convoy/cargo inventory delivery belongs to P4.
